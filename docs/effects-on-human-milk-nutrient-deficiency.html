Section 6 Effects on human milk nutrient deficiency

Relative risks (RRs) of nutrient deficiency, pooled intervention vs. control, estimated by TMLE within each trial and visit. A sample is deficient when its concentration is below the 10th percentile of the age-specific reference distribution from the Mothers, Infants, and Lactation Quality (MILQ) study; RR < 1 means less deficiency with the intervention. nY of N samples were deficient. Components with fewer than five deficient samples in a trial and visit were not analyzed. Q (pval_adj) is FDR-adjusted within visit and outcome group.
