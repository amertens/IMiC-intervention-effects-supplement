IMiC intervention effects on human milk: online resource

Overview

This online resource accompanies the manuscript:

Nutritional interventions’ impacts on human milk: three trials in low-resource settings (Dailey-Chwalibóg, Mertens et al., 2026). International Milk Composition (IMiC) Consortium.

It reports the analytical outputs behind the manuscript in more detail than the printed article can hold: interactive forest and volcano plots, searchable and filterable estimate tables, longitudinal trajectory plots, MILQ-referenced deficiency analyses, BMI-stratified subgroup analyses, reproducible pathway-enrichment results, and the cross-compartment analyses in maternal and infant blood.

The online resource has ten numbered sections, preceded by this unnumbered overview and followed by a page recording the R session that produced it. The paper cites Sections 1–8 by number in its Methods, Results, and figure legends. §9 (reproducible pathway and enrichment analyses) holds the full results behind the manuscript’s pathway tables (Tables S2–S7), and §10 (cross-compartment analyses in maternal and infant blood) those behind its cross-compartment tables (Tables S8–S11) and main-text Fig. 6D; the Results cite both as “the online resource”.

Table and figure numbers throughout this online resource match the manuscript’s supplementary materials (Tables S1–S11 and Figs. S1–S5). The supplementary figures appear here as follows:

0.2 Methods recap

We conducted a harmonized cross-trial analysis of individual participant human-milk data from 640 mother–infant dyads drawn from three randomized controlled trials in one low-income and two lower-middle-income countries: MISAME-III (Burkina Faso; prenatal and/or postnatal balanced energy–protein supplementation), Mumta-LW (Pakistan; postnatal BEP and BEP+infant azithromycin), and ELICIT (Tanzania; maternal nicotinamide during lactation with independent infant antimicrobial arms). Each trial contributed its own intent-to-treat population, restricted to dyads with at least one HM measurement contributing to IMiC.

Primary outcomes are macronutrients and micronutrients; secondary are HMOs and bioactive proteins; tertiary are targeted metabolites (Biocrates MxP Quant 500); exploratory are untargeted metabolomics, untargeted proteomics, and microbiome composition. All components were expressed in native assay units, then standardized to study × visit z-scores before TMLE, so effect estimates on the z-score scale are mean differences in SD units. FDR correction was applied within each outcome group × study × visit. Complete methods are in the manuscript’s Materials and methods.

The enrichment and cross-compartment sections use two different mass tolerances, which are not interchangeable. Pathway and compound annotation in §9 (Mummichog) uses a 10 ppm database tolerance against the human_mfn library, whereas cross-platform feature matching in §10 uses a ±25 ppm tolerance between two measured masses (ppm = |m₁ − m₂| / m × 10⁶ ≤ 25).

0.3 How to read interactive figures

In the web version, forest and volcano plots are interactive plotly widgets. Hovering over a point shows the component name, study, visit, ATE, 95% CI, and FDR-adjusted Q-value, and the mode bar at the top right of each plot zooms, pans, lasso-selects, or exports a PNG. Each plot is paired with a searchable table of the same data, with column filters and sortable columns.

Plots drawn for this online resource use the colorblind-safe Okabe–Ito palette. Significance levels, studies, BMI strata, and randomized contrasts also differ in symbol shape or line type, so the plots can be read with a color-vision deficiency or in grayscale.

If a data file that a section depends on is missing, the section prints a notice naming the file in place of its output. Rerunning the analysis code (see Reproducing the online resource in the repository README) regenerates these files.

0.4 Machine-readable data files

Four supplementary tables (S1, S3, S6, and S7) were too large to print in full, so the supplementary materials give a short caption for each and point here for the complete data; Table S1 comes in wide and long formats. A sixth file holds the native-unit intervention effects for every targeted metabolite. Each is a plain CSV that can be downloaded directly:
