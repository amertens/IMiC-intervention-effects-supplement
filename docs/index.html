IMiC intervention effects on human milk: online resource

IMiC intervention effects on human milk: online resource

Overview

This online resource accompanies Dailey-Chwalibóg, Mertens et al. (2026), “Nutritional interventions’ impacts on human milk: three trials in low-resource settings”, from the International Milk Composition (IMiC) Consortium. It holds the full results tables, interactive views of the estimates, and analyses that are not in the article.

Estimates and plots

Effects are average treatment effects (ATEs) estimated by targeted maximum likelihood estimation (TMLE) with adjustment for baseline covariates, in standard deviations of each component’s study- and visit-specific z-score unless a table gives native units. Q is the Benjamini–Hochberg FDR-adjusted P value, computed within outcome group, study, and visit.

In volcano plots, points with Q ≥ 0.05 are black (squares if P < 0.05), and points with Q < 0.05 take the color and symbol of their component category; the gray line marks P = 0.05 and the green line the FDR threshold of each panel. In forest plots of randomized arms, each arm has its own color and the symbol marks significance: open circle P ≥ 0.05, open triangle P < 0.05, filled circle Q < 0.05. Hover over a point for its values. Tables can be searched, sorted, and filtered by column.

Data files

The complete versions of the supplementary tables printed in part, as CSV files:

  • table_s1_primary_secondary_native_units.csv: Table S1, one row per trial, visit, and outcome: per-arm sample size and raw distribution side by side, with the native-unit ATE, 95% CI, P, and Q (627 rows; Section 8.2.2)
  • targeted_metabolites_native_units.csv: native-unit arm means and effects for the 581 targeted metabolites, one row per trial, visit, and metabolite (3,911 rows; Section 8.2.1)
  • table_s3_tertiary_msea_full.csv: Table S3, targeted-metabolome MSEA, all rows (463 rows; Section 9.2)
  • table_s6_mummichog_full.csv: Table S6, untargeted-metabolome Mummichog enrichment, pathways with P < 0.05 (505 rows; Section 9.4)
  • table_s7_proteomics_go_full.csv: Table S7, proteome GO Biological Process enrichment, terms with P < 0.05 and Storey q < 0.20 (182 rows; Section 9.5)
