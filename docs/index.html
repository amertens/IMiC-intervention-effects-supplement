Overview

This online appendix is the complete, searchable companion to the manuscript:

Nutritional interventions’ impacts on human milk: three trial analyses from low-resource settings (Dailey-Chwalibóg, Mertens et al., 2025). International Milk Composition (IMiC) Consortium.

It provides every analytical output that supports the main manuscript — interactive forest and volcano plots, searchable estimate tables (filter and CSV-download enabled), longitudinal trajectory visualisations, MILQ-anchored deficiency analyses, BMI-stratified subgroup analyses, and pathway-level enrichment results — at a level of detail that does not fit in the printed article PDF.

The appendix is organised into eight numbered sections matching the references in the printed paper (Sections 1–8), preceded by this unnumbered overview and followed by a reproducibility appendix.

0.2 Methods recap

We conducted a harmonised individual-participant data meta-analysis of human milk from 640 mother–infant dyads drawn from three randomised controlled trials in one low- and two lower-middle-income country settings: MISAME-III (Burkina Faso; prenatal and/or postnatal balanced energy–protein supplementation), Mumta-LW (Pakistan; postnatal BEP and BEP+infant azithromycin), and ELICIT (Tanzania; maternal nicotinamide during lactation with independent infant antimicrobial arms). Each trial contributed its own intent-to-treat population, restricted to dyads with at least one HM measurement contributing to IMiC.

Primary outcomes are macronutrients and micronutrients; secondary are HMOs and bioactive proteins; tertiary are targeted metabolites (Biocrates MxP Quant 500); exploratory are untargeted metabolomics, untargeted proteomics, and microbiome composition. All components were expressed in native assay units, then standardised to study × visit z-scores before TMLE. Effect estimates from the primary chapter therefore represent mean differences in SD units, with FDR correction applied within each outcome group × study × visit. The complete methods are in the manuscript and its Methods supplement.

The full outcomes list — every milk component analysed in this supplement, grouped by prespecified outcome class with per-study availability — is now in §2 Outcomes and human milk component distributions, so that the manuscript Methods reference “A list of all outcomes and descriptions is available in the Online Appendix (Section 2)” resolves to a numbered section rather than an unnumbered overview.

0.3 How to read interactive figures

In the HTML build, forest and volcano plots are rendered as plotly widgets. Hover any point to reveal the component name, study, visit, ATE, 95% CI, and FDR-adjusted q-value. Plotly’s mode bar (top-right of each plot) allows zoom, pan, lasso-select, and PNG export. Every plot is paired with a DT::datatable containing the same underlying data with column filters, free-text search, sortable columns, and CSV/Excel download buttons.

Where a section relies on an upstream data artefact that is not present in the current build, the section prints an inline notice naming the missing file rather than failing. Re-run the upstream analysis pipeline (see Reproducing the supplement in the repository README) to regenerate those artefacts.
