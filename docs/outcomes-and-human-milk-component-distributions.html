IMiC supplementary material

Section 2 Outcomes and human milk component distributions

The section has two halves. The first is the outcomes list referenced from the manuscript Methods (“A list of all outcomes and descriptions is available in the Online Appendix (Section 2)”): a searchable inventory of every milk component analysed in the IMiC harmonised dataset, grouped by prespecified outcome class and annotated with per-study availability. From MILQ-overlay density plots onward, the second half describes the distribution of each component, stratified by study, visit, and randomised intervention arm, and corresponds to Figure S2 of the printed Word supplement.

Read together, these two views establish what was measured, where, and what the raw distribution looks like for each component, before the modelled intervention-effect estimates in the subsequent sections.

2.1 Outcomes list

The table below enumerates every milk component analysed in this supplement, grouped by the prespecified outcome class used throughout the manuscript (primary: macronutrients, micronutrients, B-vitamins; secondary: HMOs, bioactive proteins; tertiary: targeted metabolites from the Biocrates MxP Quant 500 panel). Per-study availability indicators show in which of the three IMiC trials each component was measured.

Use the column filters at the top of the table to look up a specific component, or search by name in the upper-right.

The full targeted-metabolite metadata (Biocrates panel categories, with per-study availability) is reproduced as a focused subsection in §6 Targeted-metabolite metadata and reductions in HM nutrient deficiency so that the metadata called out in the manuscript Fig. 5A legend (“metadata in Online Supplement 6”) lives at that section’s anchor.

2.2 Distributions

The remainder of this section shows the unadjusted distribution of each measured human milk component, stratified by study, visit, and randomized intervention arm. Two views follow: (i) density plots overlaid by intervention arm, and (ii) per-component boxplots faceted by study and visit. Density plots include grey and red reference lines at the 50th and 10th percentiles of the MILQ age-specific reference distribution where applicable. Asterisks indicate FDR-corrected statistical significance of the pooled intervention vs. control contrast for that component–visit (\* q\<0.05, \\ q\<0.01, \\\* q\<0.001). Y-axes are log-transformed where the underlying distribution is markedly right-skewed (most micronutrients and metabolites).

Figure S2. IMiC distributions of HM nutrients relative to MILQ reference standards. Violin plots of native-unit concentrations for each primary component with a significant intervention effect (energy density, fat, protein, B-vitamins B1/B2/B3/B5/B6/B7/B12, vitamin A, α- and γ-tocopherol, selenium, copper), stratified by study (E: ELICIT, Mi: MISAME-III, Mu: Mumta-LW), visit, and intervention arm (blue = control, orange = pooled intervention). Horizontal grey and red reference lines mark the 50th and 10th percentiles of the age-specific MILQ reference distribution; asterisks mark intervention-vs-control significance after FDR correction (* q < 0.05, ** q < 0.01, *** q < 0.001). Y-axes are log-transformed.
